What is a Loan Against Property?

A Loan Against Property (LAP) is a secured loan wherein an eligible property owner pledges a freehold residential, commercial, or industrial property as mortgage collateral to raise long-term, high-value funds.

Unlike unsecured personal or business loans, LAP offers substantially larger loan limits (up to ₹1.25 Crores with BKL Association) and longer repayment tenures, resulting in lower, more manageable monthly EMIs.

Key Underwriting Factors:

  • Property Valuation: Lenders sanction between 50% to 70% of the property's appraised market value (Loan-to-Value or LTV).
  • Title & Legal Clearance: The property must have clear, marketable title deeds without encumbrances or unresolved disputes.
  • Without ITR & GST Options: Self-employed business owners can qualify through banking surrogate and turnover assessments without mandatory ITR or GST.
  • Repayment Capacity: Sanction depends on demonstrated net monthly income, cash flows, and overall debt-service ability.
Retain Complete Property Ownership:

Pledging your property does not require vacating or altering daily business operations. You retain full physical possession, tenant rentals, and operational rights as long as loan terms are honored.

Important Collateral Risk Transparency Notice

A Loan Against Property is a secured borrowing facility. Your property is pledged as security with the lending institution. In the event of persistent default or failure to maintain regular repayments in accordance with the loan agreement, the property may be subject to legal recovery and auction under applicable recovery laws (such as the SARFAESI Act). We strongly encourage all borrowers to evaluate their monthly cash flow, business revenue stability, and repayment capacity before entering into a mortgage agreement.

Assessment Criteria

LAP Eligibility & Considerations

Lending partners carry out thorough legal and technical vetting prior to loan sanction:

Property Assessment Parameters

  • Property Type: Fully constructed residential flats, individual villas, commercial office spaces, or industrial units.
  • Clear Ownership Title: Unbroken chain of title ownership deeds for a minimum of 13 to 30 years.
  • Local Authority Approvals: Approved layout sanctions, building completion certificate, and property tax payment receipts.
  • Independent Valuation: Technical inspection conducted by the bank-appointed valuer to calculate fair market value.

Applicant Financial Parameters

  • Income Consistency: Steady salary credits or minimum 2–3 years verified commercial operations with healthy cash flows.
  • Credit Score (CIBIL): Recommended 700+ score indicating responsible past credit handling.
  • Debt Burden Ratio (FOIR): Total monthly debt obligations should stay within 40%–50% of verifiable take-home income.
  • Co-Applicant Inclusion: Adding property co-owners as co-applicants is mandatory under standard mortgage guidelines.
