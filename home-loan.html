Underwriting Factors

Eligibility Guide

Lending partners evaluate several key financial and personal factors when assessing an application:

  • Applicant Age: Typically between 21 and 65 years at the time of loan maturity.
  • Employment & Income: Regular monthly salary for employees, or business vintage and cash flow for self-employed individuals.
  • Existing Monthly EMIs: Total debt commitments compared against take-home income (FOIR ratio).
  • Credit History (CIBIL): Healthy repayment track record with timely settlement of past loans or credit cards.
  • Property Details: Legal title clearance and technical appraisal. Approved building plan is not required for eligible applicants and self-construction properties.
  • Repayment Capacity: Margin money / down payment capability (typically 10% to 25% of property value).

Required Paperwork

Documentation Checklist

General documentation typically reviewed by lending institutions. Requirements vary based on whether you are salaried or self-employed:

  • Identity Proof: Valid PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
  • Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Utility Bill, or Rental Agreement.
  • Income Proof (Salaried): Latest 3 months salary slips and Form 16 / Tax returns.
  • Income Proof (Self-Employed): Business registration, or bank turnover statements (available without mandatory ITR & GST for eligible profiles).
  • Banking Records: Latest 6 months bank account statements showing regular credits.
  • Property Documents: Title deed, sale agreement, parent documents, encumbrance certificate (EC), and Patta (Building Plan Not Required for eligible cases).
