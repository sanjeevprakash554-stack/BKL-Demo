BKL Association advises on the following primary product categories across Tamil Nadu:

4. Special Programs: "Without ITR & GST" & "Plan Not Required"

BKL Association guides clients on specialized lending programs designed for self-employed individuals, traders, and property owners in Tamil Nadu:

  • Without ITR & GST Programs: Available for eligible self-employed applicants who do not possess formal Income Tax Returns (ITR) or GST registrations. Eligibility in such cases is assessed through alternative bank turnover surrogates, daily banking credits, physical business inspection, and property valuation. Sanctions remain subject to specific lender underwriting policies.
  • Home Loan — Approved Building Plan Not Required: Selected partner housing finance institutions permit residential home loans and self-construction financing without requiring an official DTCP, CMDA, or Local Body approved building plan, provided the land has clear legal ownership, valid Patta/Chitta, parent documents, and unencumbered title deeds.
